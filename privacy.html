Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This notice explains how virtualsecretary.online (“we”, “us”) uses personal data when you visit this website, email us, or telephone us. It is written to the standard of a UK GDPR privacy notice (Articles 13 and 14) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Fields in square brackets must be completed before publication.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is [Registered company name], a company registered in England and Wales (company number [Company number]), with its registered office at [Registered address].

We have not appointed a statutory data protection officer. Privacy questions and requests go to hello@virtualsecretary.online.

2. The data we use

If you browse the website

  • Technical data in server logs: IP address, date and time, pages requested, browser and device type.
  • Your cookie choice, stored in your browser. See our Cookie policy.
  • Analytics data only if you consent and analytics is switched on for this site.

This site has no account, basket or web form. We do not buy marketing lists.

If you call or email

  • Your name, telephone number, email address and anything you choose to tell us.
  • Notes we take so we can do the task: diary entries, draft letters, appointment details, and booking requests for restaurants, hotels or travel.
  • Payment references if you pay for a booking or for our time. We do not ask you to read a full card number aloud for us to store.

Information you may mention in passing

A diary or an appointment can include health information (for example a clinic booking) or other details about your private life. We use that information only to carry out the task you asked for. We do not seek special-category data for any other purpose. The lawful condition is your explicit consent (UK GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw.

3. Why we use it, and the lawful basis

PurposeLawful basis

You do not have to give us data. If you do not, we can still show you the website, but we cannot carry out a task that needs those details.

4. Who we share it with

We use processors who act on our instructions:

  • The company that hosts this website.
  • The provider of our business email and, if used, our telephone line.
  • Google Ireland / Google LLC, only if you accept analytics cookies and analytics is enabled.

When you ask us to book or to contact someone, we share the details that booking needs (typically your name and a contact number) with that restaurant, hotel, clinic, council, carrier or other provider. They become a separate controller of what they receive.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to our professional advisers under a duty of confidence. We do not sell personal data.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Hosting, email or Google services may process data outside the UK. Where the destination is not covered by UK adequacy regulations, we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the EU standard contractual clauses plus the UK Addendum, and we take the supplementary steps those terms require.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become a paid task: 12 months from our last contact with you.
  • Task and booking records: 6 years after the task ends, which matches the usual period for a contract claim in England and Wales.
  • Accounting records: 6 years after the end of the relevant tax year.
  • Server logs: up to 12 months.
  • Cookie choice: until you clear site data for this website or change your settings.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your data and for a copy of it (access);
  • correct it, or complete it;
  • erase it, or restrict what we do with it, where the law allows;
  • give you data you provided, in a portable format, where the basis is contract or consent;
  • stop processing based on legitimate interests, which we will do unless we have a compelling ground;
  • withdraw consent, including for analytics cookies and for any health details you gave us. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was already lawful.

Email hello@virtualsecretary.online. We respond within one month, or we tell you why we need longer. We may need enough detail to be sure it is your data.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF. We would like the chance to put things right first.

8. Decisions made by software

We do not make decisions about you by solely automated means, and we do not use your data for profiling.

9. Children

This website and service are for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, email hello@virtualsecretary.online and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top. A change that affects a task already under way will also be drawn to your attention by email or on the call.
